# KisanSetu / UNNATI

## Farmer records, public services, and local market coordination

UNNATI is a bilingual web application for farm profiles, crop and land records, marketplace listings, public scheme discovery, and complaint tracking. The live product is not validated for real farmer data or lending decisions.

### Potential buyer

Local FPOs, farmer-service providers, or institutions that need a simpler way to coordinate verified farmer services. This buyer and willingness to pay are unvalidated.

### Trust boundaries

- Farmer records stay scoped to the farmer's account; bank access is disabled until farmer-chosen, institution-specific consent is designed and reviewed.
- Scheme and loan outputs are educational demos and do not determine benefits, eligibility, credit, or approval.
- Account data export exists; uploaded file contents are not included.
- No real farmer, financial, health, or Aadhaar data should be used in demos or test fixtures.

### Possible revenue path

Validate a paid, fixed-scope FPO onboarding/support pilot first. A recurring B2B subscription is only a later hypothesis after a real buyer, service capacity, data policy, and renewal need are proven. No price or revenue is verified.

### Next evidence

Conduct field interviews with farmers and local service buyers; verify official scheme sources; restore and monitor the API host; review data handling with qualified counsel; secure one written pilot decision before enabling paid use.

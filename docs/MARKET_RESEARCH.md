# KisanSetu / UNNATI market research

**Checked:** 2026-11-17. Pricing is a public-source snapshot; where no subscription price is published, say so. Public reviews are individual reports and can be incomplete or outdated. This desk research is not farmer or buyer validation.

## Target user and problem

Small farmers and local farmer groups who need to find public scheme information, keep a farm profile, and discover local buyers or services without treating a software score as an official eligibility decision. Likely paying buyers are FPOs, agri-service organizations, or institutions, but no buyer interviews or paid pilot are verified.

## Positioning hypothesis

**KisanSetu can become a privacy-first coordination workspace for a local FPO or agri-service partner: farmer-managed records, public scheme discovery, and transparent buyer conversations, without implying that a demo score predicts credit, benefits, or government approval.** This is a hypothesis to validate with field interviews.

## Buyer questions

1. Which scheme, record, or buyer coordination task is most often delayed today?
2. Who would pay: an FPO, service provider, institution, or farmer? What budget and procurement path exist?
3. Which records must never be shared with a lender, buyer, or service provider?
4. What official source and update date are needed before scheme information can be trusted?
5. Would a partner pay for onboarding/support before anyone pays for a recurring software subscription?
